Cold at the bottom, hot at the top? You probably need a powerflush.

Sludge and corrosion build up inside radiators and pipework over time, especially in older Dorset properties on hard water. A powerflush restores circulation and often adds years to the life of a boiler that was heading for replacement. We will tell you honestly whether a flush will fix your problem or whether you are looking at a system fault instead.

Most Popular Questions

How much does central heating installation cost in Dorchester?

It depends on the size of the property, the number of radiators and whether the existing pipework can be reused. We survey the property, explain the options and give you a fixed written quote before any work begins.

How long does a full installation take?

Most domestic installations take between two and five days depending on the size of the property and how much pipework needs replacing. We give you a realistic timescale up front and work to it.

What is powerflushing and do I need it?

Powerflushing forces a cleaning solution through the system at high velocity to clear sludge and corrosion. You may need it if radiators have cold patches at the bottom, take a long time to warm up, or if the water is black when you bleed them. We check before recommending it.

Can you install smart heating controls?

Yes. We fit and configure modern smart thermostats and zoned controls, and we show you how to use them properly, which is where most of the savings actually come from.
