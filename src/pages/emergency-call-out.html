While you wait for us, do these three things

  1. 1. Turn off the water. Find your internal stopcock, usually under the kitchen sink, and turn it clockwise until it stops. This alone limits most of the damage from a burst pipe.
  2. 2. Turn off the boiler, and the power if water is near electrics. Switch the boiler off at the fused spur. If water is anywhere near sockets or wiring, turn the electricity off at the consumer unit and do not touch anything wet.
  3. 3. Contain and call. Buckets and towels down, move what you can, then call {{phone}} and describe exactly what you see.
