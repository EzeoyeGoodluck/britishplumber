Boiler stopped in cold weather? It may be a frozen condensate pipe.

In a Dorset cold snap this is the single most common boiler failure, and it is often something you can fix yourself in ten minutes. If your boiler has locked out and you can hear gurgling, the external condensate pipe has probably frozen. Pour warm, not boiling, water along the outside pipe, then reset the boiler. Most Popular Questions

How much does a boiler repair cost in Dorchester?

It depends on the fault and the part. We diagnose first, tell you the cost, and let you decide before we proceed. You will never get a bill with something on it you did not agree to.

Do you repair all boiler makes?

We work on all the major domestic makes and models. If your boiler is very old and parts are no longer manufactured, we tell you honestly rather than charging you to search for something that does not exist.

My boiler keeps losing pressure, what does that mean?

Repeated pressure loss usually points to a leak in the system, a failed expansion vessel or a faulty pressure relief valve. Topping it up over and over is treating the symptom. It is worth finding the cause before it damages something more expensive.

Is it worth repairing or replacing my boiler?

As a rule of thumb, if the boiler is under roughly ten years old and the repair is a fraction of replacement cost, repair it. If it is older, inefficient and failing repeatedly, the money is better spent on a new unit. We give you the honest arithmetic for your boiler and let you decide.
