Boiler Installations in Dorchester

There is a lot of money to be made in selling people a bigger boiler than they need. We do not do that. Every boiler installation in Dorchester we carry out starts with a proper look at the property, the size of the home, the number of bathrooms, the water pressure and how you actually live. Only then do we recommend a boiler. Sometimes that is a straightforward combi swap. Sometimes a system boiler with a cylinder is a far better answer, particularly in larger or rural Dorset properties where mains pressure is poor.

Every boiler installation in Dorchester is carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers. We notify building control, register the manufacturer warranty in your name and hand you the paperwork, so you are never left chasing a certificate. Your old boiler and all the packaging leave with us at no extra cost.

We give you a fixed written quote after a free survey, so the price you are told is the price you pay. There is no day-two discovery of an extra you were never warned about. As a veteran owned business, that straightforwardness is the whole point, and it is why our customers recommend us.

Most Popular Questions

How much does a new boiler cost in Dorchester?

The cost depends on the boiler type, the make, and whether it is a straight swap or a system conversion. We give you a fixed written quote after a free survey and always show more than one option.

How long does the work take?

A straightforward combi replacement is usually a one day job. A conversion, relocation or full system change typically takes two to three days. We confirm the timescale in the quote.

Do you register the warranty and notify building control?

Yes. We register the manufacturer warranty in your name and notify building control through the Gas Safe scheme. You receive the certificate and paperwork without chasing anyone.

Will you take my old boiler away?

Yes. The old boiler, all packaging and any waste leave with us at no extra cost.
